Results: Tax Strategy Comparison

Below are four different tax lot accounting methods. The best method (lowest tax) is highlighted in green.

FIFO (First In, First Out)

Matches your sale against the oldest purchases first. This is the IRS default method.

LIFO (Last In, First Out)

Matches your sale against the newest purchases first.

HIFO (Highest In, First Out)

Matches your sale against the highest-cost purchases first. Often the most tax-efficient.
